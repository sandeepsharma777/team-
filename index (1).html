A campus where no good idea dies for lack of a team.

Every college has students with ideas, skills to offer, and no reliable way to find each other. TeamSync exists to make "who do I even ask?" a solved problem — turning scattered student ambition into visible, collaborative, mentored projects.

01

Problem → Solution

Student collaboration is stuck in group chats and luck.

Here's what's actually broken today, and how TeamSync closes the gap.

The problem

  • Great ideas stall because students can't find teammates with complementary skills.
  • Hackathon teams get formed last-minute, often mismatched, based on who's simply available.
  • Projects live and die in WhatsApp threads with no structure, tasks, or history.
  • Mentorship is informal and depends entirely on who you already happen to know.
  • Colleges can't see or support the innovation actually happening on their own campus.
→

The TeamSync solution

  • Skill-based profiles make it easy to search and discover the right teammate, fast.
  • An idea board lets anyone post a project and get matched with people who fit the gaps.
  • A shared team workspace keeps tasks, docs, and chat in one persistent place per project.
  • A mentor directory turns "ask around" into a simple, bookable request.
  • Innovation cells get a dashboard to track and support every active student project.

01

Matching + workspace + mentorship, in one continuous flow

02

Business model

Free for students, paid where the value scales.

Revenue comes from the organizations and power users who get outsized value from the platform, not from gating basic team formation.

Freemium

Free core, paid upgrade

Profile creation, team formation, and basic workspace tools are free for every student, always. This keeps the network large and active.

TeamSync Pro

Optional individual upgrade

A paid tier for power users: priority matching, advanced portfolio analytics, and early access to mentor slots and featured hackathons.

B2B licensing

Innovation cell dashboard

Colleges and TBIs pay for a cohort-management dashboard: visibility into active projects, participation tracking, and outcome reporting.

Event hosting

Hackathon hosting fees

Organizations running hackathons or ideation events pay a hosting fee to use TeamSync for team formation, submissions, and judging.

Sponsored slots

Featured mentor placement

Companies and professionals can sponsor featured mentor slots to build visibility and goodwill with the student talent pipeline.

Talent access

Recruiter / scouting access

Startups and companies pay for structured access to the showcase gallery to scout project-proven student talent early.

Go-to-market strategy

Start on one campus. Prove the loop. Expand deliberately.

A phased rollout that earns trust with one dense, engaged community before spreading outward.

PHASE 1 — CAMPUS PILOT

Launch with campus ambassadors at one college

Recruit student ambassadors and innovation-cell partners at a single campus to seed the first wave of profiles and projects.

PHASE 2 — CITY NETWORK

Partner with nearby colleges

Expand to other colleges in the same city, using inter-college hackathons as the natural entry point for cross-campus teams.

PHASE 3 — MENTOR & SPONSOR ONBOARDING

Bring in mentors and corporate partners

Onboard faculty, alumni, and industry mentors at scale, and open sponsorship slots for companies wanting early talent access.

PHASE 4 — NATIONAL EXPANSION

Scale pan-India with incubator tie-ups

Expand to colleges nationally, partnering with incubators and TBIs to plug TeamSync into the broader student-innovation ecosystem.
